OUR SERVICES

MINING &
EXPLORATION

From exploration planning to drilling supervision, quality management and geotechnical investigations — engineering expertise throughout the mining project lifecycle.

01

Mine exploration drilling project management

From grassroots to resource definition stage, efficient drilling management leads exploration projects to success. Pangea consultants optimize and standardize the drilling work in accordance with NI 43-101 and JORC code.

02

Project planning, application, follow-up

Drilling is a dynamic process which might be influenced by many external factors, from drilling contractor and its employees to weather/terrain and environmental/social conditions. Pangea consultants prepare terms and conditions dedicated to the project at tendering stage, make timeline and monthly progress plan matching with the client’s needs and project requirements, advise the most efficient drilling method and make regular inspections on-site.

03

Total quality management (Cost/production analysis, quality control)

TQM covers the drilling project in all aspects. Technical analysis accompanied by environmental and social impact analysis is the base for project evaluation. Pangea consultants offer to improve the standards of its clients’ drilling projects to internationally accepted level. The concept consists of analysis, evaluation, corrective preventive activity and providing sustainability.

04

Mine exploration drilling

Besides consultancy services, Pangea also offers diamond core drilling contracting services with the contribution of solution partners. Efficient drilling requires not only technical ability and capability but also cooperation and good communication between the client and the contractor. Pangea draws on experience gained by managing drilling operations across more than 20 countries on 4 continents since 2000.

05

Geological, hydrogeological and geotechnical investigation drilling

Geotechnical investigation drilling takes place at late stages of mineral exploration for slope stability of open cast pits, determination of soil and rock physical characteristics where plant, tailing dam, etc. is constructed. Pangea provides geotechnical drilling services with drill rigs capable of in-situ tests.

06

Soil and rock laboratory testing / Final Report

Samples are tested at accredited laboratories, and the geotechnical investigation report is finalized by combining data from field investigation, drilling, in-situ testing and laboratory tests.
