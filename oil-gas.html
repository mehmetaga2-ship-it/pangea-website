OIL &

Procurement management, pipeline construction equipment, welding solutions and testing support for oil and gas projects.

In addition to the mining field, PANGEA provides equipment and services for the oil & gas sector. Drawing on its management team’s experience, PANGEA studies local and international project requirements and connects clients with suitable manufacturers, products and suppliers, with an emphasis on competitive pricing and service.
